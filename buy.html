Before you buy

Questions about billing

Is $15 really the whole price?

Yes. One payment of $15, and Element 26 is yours to keep — there's no monthly charge, no renewal, and no upgrade tier sitting behind it. The licence covers one person.

Do I pay again for updates?

No. Updates come with it. "Forever" means the version you bought keeps working and keeps improving, not that you bought a snapshot.

What exactly do I get?

The app itself. Element 26 runs in your browser and installs to your home screen, where it opens full-screen with its own icon and works offline. There's no app store, no review queue, and no separate download per platform.

What happens right after I pay?

You go straight through to the app. You give it a name, it hands you a User ID and a recovery key to save, and you're training. That's the entire setup — there's no email confirmation to wait for.

What if I lose my phone?

Your User ID and recovery key are what identify your training, which is why the app tells you to save them at sign-up. Keep those and your history comes back; your whole account also exports as a file you can keep yourself.

Do I need an account to train?

You need one to keep your training tied to you, but it costs you a name and nothing else. No email address, no password, and no signing in with anyone else's account. If you'd rather nothing left the device, everything can stay local.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Your whole account exports as a file you keep, and the same file restores it. History also prints as a report you can hand to a coach or a physio.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Everything you log is written locally first, and syncing is something the app does when it can rather than something it needs to function. Gym basements are the normal case, not the edge case.
